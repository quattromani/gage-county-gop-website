# Original-site content review

Reviewed October 26, 2026, against the [public inventory](../source-snapshot/inventory.json): 24 captured page URLs and 36 downloaded assets, with no recorded capture errors. This is an editorial migration review. It does not independently verify officeholders, candidate affiliations, governing rules, image rights, or payment destinations.

The site contains useful party records, photographs, contact information, and policy text. Its main weakness is the absence of a clear distinction between current information, historical records, organizational rules, and unfinished template material. The redesign should preserve the evidence while making those distinctions visible.

## Content to carry forward

Retain the mission from `/vision`, the complete platform from `/nebraska-platform`, the history from `/history`, the leadership portraits and published roster, committee and convention records, constitution scans, event photographs, contact details, and specific social links. Preserve policy wording as the party's source text; distinguish it from independently verified factual guidance. Asset capture establishes availability, not a new license or permission to reuse third-party imagery.

Import the existing Gage County GOP voter-card project with its source and provenance intact. Keep candidate corrections upstream and retain its accessibility, calendar, sharing, and offline behavior. Add `https://partyreveal.com/` as a clearly labeled external navigation destination, as requested. A link does not import that site's content or transfer authority for its claims to this repository.

## Remaining confirmations

The public capture cannot supply private Squarespace drafts, original layered artwork, form submissions, administrative settings, unpublished files, or asset licenses. The meeting year and future venue, current leadership confirmation, governing-summary reconciliation, donation recipient, and uncertain event dates remain content-owner questions. Preserve these limits in maintenance records and avoid filling them with assumptions.
